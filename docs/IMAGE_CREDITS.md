## What these images mean

STELE Residences is a fictional architectural concept. Unsplash photographs on the site are mood, material, landscape or typology references; they are not photographs of a STELE building, selected development site, or verified amenity. Image alt text and surrounding copy label them as references. The aerial-style image is used as general city / river context, not a location pin or property map.

Photographs are downloaded from Unsplash's public photo CDN, cropped to fit their slots, and re-encoded as local WebP derivatives. Source photo pages are included for the three interior images replaced during the 2026-10-10 visual consistency pass. Legacy image CDN identifiers are retained below where the original contributor page was not independently verified during the earlier asset pass.
